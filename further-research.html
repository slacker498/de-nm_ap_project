Limitations and Further Research

  • One crucial limitation this research uncovered was that a direct replacement for an RLC circuit is impossible. If using active filters, one needs to consider the use case, whether for filtering circuits or surge suppression circuits, as well as the frequency the circuit would be working with, factors not considered directly by KVL-based or transfer function second-order models.
  • In conducting further research, we will explore the use of Antoniou Gyrators, circuit segments we discovered later in our research, to directly replace the inductors, without changing the R and C for a series RLC circuit. It would also help analyse the charge response directly, unlike the voltage response used in our modifications.
  • Also, further comparisons would be made for other second-order Sallen-Key topology pass circuits to consider which could serve as a more generalised solution to standard RLC series circuits.
